How to use Ojoola
My profile: On the Chats page, tap your picture next to the search icon.

Large picture: Open a profile and tap the big picture. Tap anywhere to go back.

Other people: Tap their picture, name or message to see their profile.

Reactions: Double tap a message to react.

Voice notes: Tap the microphone to record and tap again to send.

Edit profile: Open My Profile, then Edit Profile, change your details and save.
